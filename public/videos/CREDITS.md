# Video credits

All clips from **Coverr** (https://coverr.co) under the Coverr License:
free for commercial and personal use, no attribution required.

Encoded here at **1920x1080**, H.264, no audio, faststart, CRF 27 capped at
2.2 Mbps (3 Mbps for the scrub variants). The cap is deliberate: these sit
under a heavy veil as backgrounds, so bitrate spent on detail the veil hides
is wasted. Colour grading happens in CSS at render time.

An earlier pass re-encoded these to 1600px wide at 130-880 kbps, which was
visibly blocky — `flight.mp4` was 130 kbps for a full-bleed background. The
Coverr sources are 1920x1080 at ~4.5 Mbps; these are pulled from those.

## Honesty note

This is stock footage, not Rami's own. It is used as atmosphere behind the
3D model and type, never captioned or framed as his work. If any clip is ever
placed so a visitor would read it as documentation of his own projects,
replace it with real footage instead.
