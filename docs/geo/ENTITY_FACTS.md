## Confidence labels

When adding a fact to the content system, assign one internal confidence label:

- `verified-current` — checked against a current first-party or official source.
- `reviewed` — supported by the canonical CV or existing public site but needs current re-verification.
- `provisional` — plausible from repository or historical evidence but not ready for public copy.
- `withheld` — known privately but not cleared for public use.
- `rejected` — contradicted, inaccurate, or intentionally removed.

Only `verified-current` and explicitly approved `reviewed` facts may become new indexable claims.
