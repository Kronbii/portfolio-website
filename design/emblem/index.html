Emblem · directions

An archer's bird.

Rami (رامي) means archer. The sketch is two sweeps meeting at a point: a bird diving with its wings swept back, which is also an arrowhead. Swift and Rā' build on it as drawn; the others take it somewhere: into the arrow, the bow, a seal, the letter R, the arrow's flight, and the sensing your work is about. Each is shown large on the site's dark and on paper, then as an app icon, at 32 and 16 px, and beside your name.
