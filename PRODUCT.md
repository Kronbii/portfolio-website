# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Researchers, professors, and engineering teams evaluating Rami Kronbi's technical depth and research direction.
- Recruiters, collaborators, and institutions looking for evidence of applied robotics, computer vision, embedded systems, and public-interest technology work.
- Engineers and builders searching for practical explanations, reusable open-source work, and implementation lessons.
- Search engines and AI answer engines that need a consistent, attributable, machine-readable source about Rami and his projects.

## Product Purpose

Make ramikronbi.com the canonical, evidence-backed source for Rami Kronbi's public work. The site should explain what each project did, why it mattered, how it worked, what Rami contributed, what was learned, and where the underlying evidence can be inspected.

Success means a human or AI system can connect Rami's name to his real fields and projects without relying on inflated claims, duplicated biographies, or disconnected social profiles.

## Positioning

The portfolio is not a gallery of screenshots. It is an engineering record centered on consequential systems: robotics, embedded perception, computer vision, control, edge AI, and technology built for real institutional or public needs.

## Operating Context

- The existing homepage and current visual system are established and remain authoritative.
- New project, writing, and topic pages extend that system without redesigning or rewriting the existing site.
- The canonical sources are public repositories and demos, the canonical 2026 research CV, existing first-party portfolio content, and clearly attributable institutional sources.
- Content may later be syndicated to Medium and DEV, but ramikronbi.com remains the canonical origin.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- New pages may be created under `/projects`, `/writing`, and `/topics`.
- Existing source files are read-only for this phase. No homepage, navigation, global-style, metadata-route, sitemap, robots, package, or configuration edits are authorized.
- Every public claim must be supported by a named source. Conflicting claims must be narrowed, qualified, or withheld.
- Draft material may be built for review, but draft routes must use `noindex, nofollow` and must not appear in public indexes.
- Confidential employer, defense, private-repository, and sensitive partner work must not be expanded into public copy.
- Rami is the author and subject. Claude Code is the implementation tool. Codex owns editorial direction, factual review, and acceptance.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the incumbent dark, bold, typographic, cinematic site identity.
- Preserve Zalando Sans, Fraunces, the current black/neutral palette, and the controlled red accent already expressed by the repository.
- Pages should feel authored, technical, direct, and specific. Avoid generic personal-brand language, marketing superlatives, and AI-sounding filler.
- Real project imagery and real diagrams are preferred over decorative generated imagery.

## Product Principles

1. Canonical first: the website owns the definitive version; syndication points back to it.
2. Evidence before reach: accurate, sourceable pages are more valuable than a larger index of questionable claims.
3. Show the mechanism: explain architecture, constraints, tradeoffs, failures, and measurements instead of relying on adjectives.
4. Separate authored work from participation: make Rami's role explicit and credit collaborators.
5. Publish in layers: verified pages are indexable; uncertain pages remain private review drafts until approved.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic headings, keyboard-visible focus, meaningful alternative text, and reduced-motion support are required.
- Article body text must remain comfortably readable on mobile and desktop.
- Arabic names and terms must preserve correct Unicode and direction where used.
